Discover the Traditional Dances of Jodhpur

Immerse yourself in the vibrant and diverse dance traditions of Jodhpur. From graceful movements to energetic performances, explore the dances that embody the cultural spirit of this historic city.

Chari Dance

History:

Chari dance is a traditional dance performed by women, showcasing their skill in balancing clay pots on their heads while dancing. It is a significant part of Jodhpur's cultural rituals and celebrations.

Description:

In this dance, performers balance multiple clay pots (chari) on their heads while executing intricate dance steps. It demonstrates both grace and strength and is often performed during community festivals and weddings.

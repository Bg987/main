Experience the soulful melodies and traditional rhythms of Jodhpur. These songs capture the essence of the region’s cultural heritage and offer a glimpse into the musical traditions of this historic city.

Moomal

History:

The song “Moomal” is a famous Rajasthani folk song that tells the story of the legendary Moomal, a prominent figure in local folklore. This song has been a part of Jodhpur’s musical tradition for generations.

Description:

“Moomal” is known for its evocative lyrics and emotional depth. It is often performed during traditional festivals and gatherings, reflecting the rich storytelling tradition of Rajasthan.
